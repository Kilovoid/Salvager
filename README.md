# Salvager - your personal encyclopedia
### Salvager will help you trawl through the constant current of thought - and walk away with cargo worth keeping
## Main goal
The idea of this project is to provide you with your own place to write down your fleeting thoughts, memories, ideas and personal knowledge. This app's main goal is to be a very cozy personal encyclopedia, with a number of QOL features that make mapping, linking, and organizing easy. 
## Features
It is planned that the Salvager app will include:

1. "The Instrument" - a tool that makes inserting hyperlinks easy, as well as interlinking notes and info in them.
2. "The Drawer" - a feature which helps mapping any given Salvager Page quickly with places for images, tables, drawings, etc.
3. "The Hold" - you can choose to archive your Salvager Pages in an endless Hold - where you can review your old notes and choose to scrap them, restore them or save them as a relic that will not be counted in the statistics.
4. Statistics - numbers of total words, time spent on each page, growing nets of interlinked pages etc. To make notetaking a little more rewarding and pleasing. 
5. Ordinary text editing tools, but not too much - bold, italic, a limited color palette of text.... you name it
6. Tactile aesthetic - a lot of simulated physical buttons, switches, wheels and so on. 

These are the features that are supposed to be the main focus of my development and implementation of them means that the base app is done. Other features are to be added after that.
## P.S.
The app is designed to be your very personal diary - all files will be stored on your computer. It is not planned to implement any sort of log-in and sync systems. The app will only require internet to insert links from your browser quickly and no moderation or online wiki pages will be present.
